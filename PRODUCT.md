# Leafhour

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

Leafhour is a private reading room for people who want to stay with a book. Open the current passage first. Translation, notes, sources and conversation are available where the reader needs them, then yield the page back to the text.

## Product truth

- The core loop is import or choose a book, resume at the same passage, read, ask or annotate, and return to that passage.
- Original text, literal translation, interpretive translation, editorial material and personal writing have separate identities. Missing or unreviewed material keeps its real status.
- Readers can select and copy text. Opening tools, changing layout and closing conversation preserve the reading anchor.
- Personal data is private and exportable. Interface refinement never broadens model context, initiates generation, or grants access to notes or other accounts.
- The interface supports English, simplified Chinese, traditional Chinese and Japanese; paper, sage and night palettes; keyboard, touch and reduced motion.
- Persistent acceptance data belongs to the designated test account. Automated checks use isolated synthetic accounts and public-domain books.

## Voice and constraints

Follow [docs/writing.md](docs/writing.md). Be brief, specific and accurate. Place explanations at the relevant decision. Keep costs, privacy scope, errors and provenance available when needed. Preserve book text and existing data/storage compatibility identifiers.

This is an AGPL-3.0 application built with native HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules. Keep the existing domain services and interaction owners. Design tools are development tools and must stay outside the shipped reader runtime.
