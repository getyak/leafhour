Stay with a book.

Leafhour is an open-source AI reading room for people who want to spend time with a whole book.

Bring your own PDF, Markdown or text file. Read at your pace, leave a thought beside a passage, and ask for help when a sentence needs a closer look.

Read a sampleOpen reading room →

Public samples need no account. The reading interface is currently primarily in Chinese; this page explains the product in English.

Keep the text close.

The original, a translation and your own notes have separate places. Switch between original text, available literal or interpretive Chinese translations, and parallel reading without losing the passage.

AI translations are marked as drafts. Untranslated passages keep the original text and an explicit translation state. Selecting text or changing the layout does not request a model response.

When you ask the reading assistant a question, it can look up books and passages available to your account and consult web sources. Its tools do not read your private notes or write reading progress. External agents use a separate, scoped authorization.

A room of your own.

Books you upload and your personal reading records are account-scoped. Highlights and notes stay attached to the source passage. A reading calendar helps you return to a book and the questions you left there.

Reading time measures visible foreground reading; background time is paused. A timer does not decide whether you understood a book or finished a chapter.

Leafhour is published under AGPL-3.0 and can run locally or be self-hosted. The hosted site uses Supabase for accounts and storage. See the privacy and data notice in Chinese for this instance.

Before you begin.

What files can I import?

Leafhour imports PDF, Markdown (.md) and plain text (.txt). PDF text extraction may need checking against the source; image-only scans need a text layer. EPUB import is not currently supported.

Does Leafhour summarize books automatically?

The main experience is reading the text. Translation and conversation are requested by you. Original text, AI drafts and personal notes remain distinct.

Can I use my own AI agent?

Yes. The MCP adapter connects to the same reading service and requires a grant for a particular book and selected data types. Notes, activity and comment writing require their own scopes. Read the MCP integration guide.

Can I take my books and records with me?

Library export and restore are supported by the service. The current web interface does not yet expose whole-library backup controls; use a compatible client or contact support@leafhour.com. Export does not require a subscription. Unsynced drafts remain in the browser where you wrote them.

Can I read without paying for AI?

Public samples can be read without an account or a model request. AI translation and conversation require a configured model service. Self-hosters provide their own services and bear any provider charges; this page does not promise unlimited AI use.

Can friends read together?

You can prepare a gift letter with selected reading material. A live shared reading room with online presence is a future idea, not a current feature.

Open by design.

Inspect the source, run your own instance, or follow the work on GitHub. Source code and development documentation are public; uploaded books and personal records are runtime data, separate from the repository.
