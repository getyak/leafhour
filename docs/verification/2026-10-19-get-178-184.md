## Repair batch 2 (freeze3 review) — defects closed

1. **P1 owner identity mismatch** — `createPaletteLibrary` now distinguishes a
   known owner source (a getter, including known guest via `null`) from unknown
   identity (no getter, support pages). An incompatible session identity is
   rejected **before `/api/library` and again at adoption** — no request ever
   carries identity B under an owner A context, and a known guest can never
   adopt a newly authenticated different owner. Unknown-identity pages still
   bootstrap/recheck the session (private adoption rechecks identity) and clear
   private cache synchronously before fresh verification on reopen. Exact
   `/api/library`, no-store, generation and owner fences retained.
   Tests: "a known owner A context never requests or adopts a session identity
   B", "a known guest cannot adopt a newly authenticated different owner",
   "unknown identity on support pages still adopts the current session owner".

   side; the palette side synchronously clears rendered DOM and fences pending
   generations on `reader:session-changed`, and the mismatch guard (1) stops a
   reopen from adopting B while `readerSession` still reports A.
   Test: "the reader session-changed event synchronously clears DOM-visible
   data and fences pending generations".
3. **P2 stale request state** — stale tickets can no longer mutate phase, data,
   error or generation; settlement and notifications bind their ticket (the old
   `drop()` idle flip is gone). Tests cover old success, old failure and old
   changed responses settling while the current load is pending.

   through the one coordinator: immersion's outside pointerdown, setFocused and

   tools cannot be re-revealed or steal focus back to the header. Chat keeps
   the typed draft/input focus; no new companion instance. Test: "the Ask AI
   handoff closes tools without bubbling re-reveal or focus theft".
6. **P2 home header wrap** — the home navigation entry is now a compact search
   icon + visible keycap with `aria-label`/`data-hint` (existing data-icon
   infrastructure); the sidebar keeps its full label. One visible keycap; no
   locale claim (GET192 owns label dictionaries).
