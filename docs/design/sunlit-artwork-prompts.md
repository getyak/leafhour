# Sunlit homepage artwork

Created 2026-10-15 with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool. Text-to-image generation, no third-party image inputs. Original PNGs are retained locally; project copies are encoded as WebP without semantic changes. These are original brand illustrations, not photographs documenting an actual reader or room. Public Ambi pages informed the spatial design research; no Ambi assets were copied.

## `app/brand-assets/sunlit-room.webp`

Transparent background: false. Exact prompt:

> Create a new original premium editorial website background photograph, very wide landscape 2:1 composition. A serene sunlit reading room with an open cream paper book and dark forest green cloth cover on a pale limestone table, occupying the lower RIGHT HALF of the image. Above and behind the book: an elegant slender dark window frame, a softly defocused lush green garden, a few beautiful fresh translucent leaves, morning sun entering diagonally from upper right, warm visible light through a very faint airy haze. The LEFT 45 percent is almost empty pale chalk ivory plaster and table, quiet negative space for dark website text, with only very subtle soft foliage shadows. Overall luminous pale neutral ivory with deep living greens; realistic photographic material detail, soft atmospheric depth, refined architecture magazine photography, believable sunlit leaves and delicate shadows, warm but no orange/yellow cast. The main book remains large and clearly recognizable on the right, the center-left remains low detail and bright. No people, no text, no readable lettering, no typography, no UI, no logo, no frame around the image, no fake lens flare, no extreme contrast. Make this feel like looking into a real peaceful green garden reading space, light and air, not a staged stock-photo desk.

## `app/brand-assets/foliage-canopy.webp`

Transparent background: true. Exact prompt:

> Photorealistic botanical cutout for a premium sunlit reading website, on a genuinely transparent background. One loose natural branch of beautiful small fresh green oval leaves, like a delicate ficus or camellia branch, entering from the upper RIGHT corner and hanging diagonally inward. Branch and leaves clustered ONLY along the top edge and right edge, with large empty transparent negative space across the left and lower areas. About 20 to 30 individually beautiful leaves of varying size, thin stems, realistic gentle curvature, luminous backlit sage and forest greens, sunlight transmitted through several leaves showing fine veins, some leaves slightly out of focus for depth. Restrained refined nature photography, organic irregular composition, NOT a dense hedge, NOT monstera, NOT palm fronds, NOT a wreath, NOT a flat vector illustration. No pot, no floor, no background, no text, no logo. Leaves should be cleanly separated against transparency so this asset can be layered over pale ivory webpage margins and used to cast soft botanical shadows.
